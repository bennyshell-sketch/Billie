# Voice samples

Recordings of **your own voice**, for cloning with ElevenLabs Instant Voice
Cloning. Billie uploads audio from this folder and nowhere else.

What works well: 5–10 minutes of clean speech, one speaker, no music or
background noise, in `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a`, `.flac`, `.ogg`, or `.webm`.

Nothing here is uploaded automatically. Cloning is a deliberate step: you pick
the files, confirm they are recordings of you, and approve the upload — and the
`voice.clone` permission has to be granted first.

Billie cannot verify whose voice is in a file. What it does instead is refuse
audio from anywhere outside this folder, ask you plainly, and log the upload.
Clone only your own voice.

The contents of this folder are git-ignored apart from this README.
